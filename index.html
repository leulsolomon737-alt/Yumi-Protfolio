About

I’m Leul—a short-form editor and motion designer based in Addis Ababa.

Most short-form editing relies on basic captions and jump cuts. I build visual retention systems for creators and educational brands: taking raw footage or dense ideas and engineering them into fast-paced reels using custom 3D motion, UI animations, and tight narrative pacing.

From script polish to high-speed rush turnarounds, I handle the full pipeline to make sure your content holds attention from second zero.
